Privacy Policy

Last updated: — placeholder text, edit this page for your store.

1. What we collect

If you create an account: your name, email, phone number, and delivery address (street, landmark, city). If you order as a guest, only what you type into the cart's delivery-details form — this is saved on your own device (browser localStorage), not on our server, unless you send it to us via WhatsApp. We also store the items in your cart on your device the same way, so it's there next time you visit.

2. How we use it

Solely to process and deliver your orders, confirm details over WhatsApp, and — if you're signed in — to pre-fill your delivery details next time so you don't retype them. We do not use your data for anything beyond running the store.

3. Cookies & local storage

NEXORA doesn't use tracking cookies or third-party ad trackers. We use your browser's local storage to remember your cart and (for guests) your delivery details, purely so the site works smoothly across visits. Clearing your browser data removes this information.

4. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Your details are shared only with the systems needed to run the store — our database/hosting provider (Supabase) and WhatsApp, when you choose to send us an order or message. We don't share data with advertisers.

5. Data security

Account data is stored with Supabase, using row-level security so only you (and the store admin) can access your profile. No online payment details are collected or stored on this website, since ordering happens over WhatsApp.

6. Your rights

You can view and update your details anytime from Profile, or remove your saved name, phone and address from Settings → Delete my saved details. To delete your account entirely, message us on WhatsApp and we'll action it.

7. Children's privacy

NEXORA is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the store grows; the "last updated" date above reflects the latest version.

9. Contact

Questions about your data? Reach us on WhatsApp: message us here.
