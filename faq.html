Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account to order?

No — you can browse and order as a guest. Add items to your cart, fill in your delivery details there, and send your order on WhatsApp. Creating an account just saves your details for next time.

How do I actually place an order?

Add items to your cart, review the total, then tap "Contact via WhatsApp" — it opens a pre-filled message with your order, items, and total for us to confirm.

What payment methods do you accept?

Payment is arranged directly over WhatsApp once your order is confirmed — [add your accepted methods here, e.g. UPI, cash on delivery, bank transfer].

How much is delivery, and is it free?

Delivery is free above the threshold shown in your cart; a flat charge applies below it. See Shipping & Returns for details.

How do I pick the right size?

On any product with sizes, tap "Size guide" next to the size options for a chest/waist/hip chart. Still unsure? Message us on WhatsApp before ordering and we'll help.

Can I cancel or change my order?

Yes — message us on WhatsApp any time before we confirm dispatch, at no charge.

What if an item arrives damaged or the wrong size?

Contact us on WhatsApp within 48 hours of delivery and we'll sort out a return or exchange. Full details in Shipping & Returns.

Is my personal information safe?

Yes — we only use your details to fulfil orders, never sell them, and you can delete your saved details anytime from Settings. See our Privacy Policy for the full picture.

I still have a question — how do I reach you?

Message us on WhatsApp: chat with us here.
